## Response style: caveman is the default (2026-09-14)

Every session in this repo answers in caveman style by default. The ruleset is
`.claude/skills/caveman/SKILL.md` — a verbatim copy of the MIT `caveman` skill
(https://github.com/JuliusBrussee/caveman); `.claude/skills/caveman/SOURCE.md`
says why it is vendored, how to update it and how to turn it off. Level
**full**. Read it and apply it without being asked.

It does NOT apply to anything that leaves the chat — commit messages, PR
bodies, docs, issue text stay normal prose — and its Auto-Clarity carve-out
drops compression for security warnings, irreversible-action confirmations and
multi-step sequences where a dropped conjunction could be misread.

Say "normal mode" or "stop caveman" to turn it off for a session.
